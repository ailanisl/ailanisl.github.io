Lots of artists have talked about their connection to the color blue. Most that I've seen are white ([Joni Mitchell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nu7aOan-lds), William Gass's [_On Being Blue_](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/156185.On_Being_Blue), Maggie Nelson's [_Bluets_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluets_(poetry_collection))). But Imani Perry took this to another level. 

This is a beautiful collection of essays on the color blue and the Afro-diaspora experience. Perry knows how to weave narratives that are both emotionally and intellectually informed. 

I was skeptical of the coherence of the project (you can make a connection anywhere if you badly want to) but the looseness of the threads are what give the book its bluesy quality. 

Some interesting threads: 
- Blue Front Cafe 
- Bluejays
- Carver's Egyptian Blue 
- Congo's flag and liberation efforts
- The blue-petaled recipes of Hoodoo
- Whitney Houston's blues
