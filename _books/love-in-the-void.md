**Notes and Quotes**

> The soul’s natural inclination to love beauty is the trap God most frequently uses in order to win it and open it the breath from on high… The beauty of the world is the mouth of a labyrinth
 
- Unwary traveller enters, gets lost
- Walks on with no expectation
- entering the center of the labyrinth, God is waiting to eat the traveller
- Come out changed, having been eaten and digested by God
- After, stays by entrance to gently push those who come near the opening

> The beauty of the world is not an attribute of matter in itself. It is a relationship of the world to our sensibility, the sensibility that depends upon the structure of our body and soul


> We want to get behind beauty, but it is only a surface. It is like a mirror that sends back our own desires for goodness. It is a sphinx, an enigma, a mystery that is painfully tantalizing.

- We should like to feed upon it but it is merely something to look at; it appears only from a certain distance.
- The great trouble in human life is that looking and eating are two different operations… only the same in the country of God.
- Vice, depravity, crime = attempt to eat beauty

> Eve began it. IF she caused humanity to be lost by eating the fruit, the opposite attitude, looking at the fruit without eating it, should be what is required to save it.

- Love of money → amounts to finality in luxury (seen as beauty)
- Love of power → amounts to desire to establish order among people, things = resulting from sense of beauty

> Forcing a certain circle into a pattern suggestive of universal beauty ≠ universe, instead hides it.
