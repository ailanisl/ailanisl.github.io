The Oedipus Complex and the dreams. Can't help being pulled to Freud. 

- Are we responsible for our own dreams?

- Are dreams more consequential and 'real' than our own reality? 
 
- What sets dreams and waking 'reality' apart? (this difference in time, linearity, logic)

 but when they start to merge, concepts and frameworks such as time break apart and begin to disintegrate. Kafka and Nakata live in the liminal world between the two.
