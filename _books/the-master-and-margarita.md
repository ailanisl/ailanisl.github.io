I bought the Vintage edition of *Master and Margarita*, translated by Diana Burgin and Katherine Tiernan O'Connor at Half-Price Books Berkeley on October 13, 2021. It was $7.99, which is an adequate price, but a little higher than what I usually will pay for a used book. 


First thoughts after finishing: "Awesome nonsense and my kind of wackiness."

That's all I wrote in my journal at the time. When I think back about it, the whole narrative feels like a fever dream. Long apartment hallways illuminated by a sheer red light. A head rolling down a Moscow street. Pontius Pilate on a palace balcony. Naked theatergoers reaching for flying money. Images from the book pop up in my mind from time to time. Something about it. 

I remember putting *Heart of a Dog* by Bulgakov on my list after reading this, but I never got around to it.
