### Surface Notes
Merging the philosophical dilemmas of cognitive science with Madhyamaka principles. 

__Cartesian anxiety__ = either we have fixed, stable ground for knowledge OR we cannot escape some sort of darkness confusion, chaos 

Marvin Minsky (Society of mind) represents the cognitive science POV of this anxiety: the world is a setting of and field foall all our experience but one that cannot be found apart from our structure, behavior, cognition

Gloominess: impossible to find independent world, only find our subjective representation
- Only gloomy if we presuppose a pregiven world, no choice but to fall back on inner representations as if they are stable ground... But we believe that the self doesn't exist

### Consequences of the "Mind as a Mirror of Nature" Ideal: 
_Madhyamaka_: grasping after a stable ground (inner or outer) = deep source of frustration and anxiety 
- Inner: essence of ego-self 
- Outer: idea of pregiven, independent world


_Absolutism_: grasping mind leads one to search for an absolute ground (inner or outer). That by its own being = support and foundation for everything

_Nihilism_: Faced with the inability to find ultimate ground, the grasping mind recoils, clings to absence of a ground by treaating everything else as illusion. 

_Groundlessness_: condition for the richly tttextured and interdependent world of human experience
- Not philosophically abtruse analysis, but found in everyday experience.
