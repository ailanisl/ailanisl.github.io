Full title: _Colorless Tsukuru Tazaki and His Years of Pilgrimmage_. I bet there's a bit of Murakami in every one of his protagonists. I relate to this one. Being an empty vessel, a container with no self. At least the feeling like I am. 

I think this is my first Murakami. I can see the mass appeal of his works. It has a distant feeling. Discordant for its Americana sense in a distinctly Japanese environs.
