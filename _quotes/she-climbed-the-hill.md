She climbed the hill again and her heart still beat without rhythm. She sought the peace of those paths at that hour, between the afternoon and the night, an invisible cicada whispering the same song...She stopped and without her footsteps she heard the silence moving. Only her body disturbed the serenity. She imagined it wihout her presence and sensed the freshness that those dead things mixed with other must have, fragility alive like at the beginning of creation.
