__"Gentildonna"__ 

She passed and left no quiver in the veins, who now  
Moving among the trees, and clinging  
    in the air she severed.  
Fanning the grass she walked on then, endures:  
<p></p>
Grey olive leaves beneath a rain-cold sky.
