I have one nagging vice in my life – that is collecting physical books, especially old used paperback books. The sunworn spines, the teal-painted edges, and the occasional stained drop of coffee.


 <img src="/assets/img/posts/used_book_haul.jpg">
<h5>A recent book haul</h5>

I engage in the act of tsundoku (buying more books than you can read and letting them accumulate in piles), which you can see in my antilibrary page. But I *do* read (albeit slowly)! And I struggled to find a good system of keeping track of them, having cycling through Goodreads, Notion spreadsheets, and my analog commonplace. So far, the latter has been most tenable and long-lasting; however, it's inflexible and hard to sort. I'm hoping this site's reading log will help me out. 


I know I could go to the library and borrow books or read ebooks through OverDrive without spending a dime, but I want to *possess* the books. Anyway, used books are perhaps the cheapest collectibles you can come across (the best places to go are the Friends of the Public Library, university town used bookstores, or those Free Little Libraries).
