### Definition: (n.)
1. A woman's purse
2. A small paperback book
3. A notebook

*One:* This website is not a place where I discuss my financial matters. Rather, it's a __purse of things that are valuable to me__: abstractions, ideas, ephemera (these are my <a href= "{{ '/pearls/' | relative_url }}" class="internal-link">pearls</a>). 

*Two:* This website is not a small paperback book, but contains a <a href= "{{ '/perusals/' | relative_url }}" class="internal-link">reading log</a>. I usually write my name and the date I completed the book on the inside flap of my paperbacks.{% include sidenote.html id="1" text="Often, I write my name and date under the books previous owner. I hope that its next owner will continue the pencilled chain" %}
--> The log just helps me keep track of them.

*Three:* This website *is* a __notebook__. It helps me sort through, organize, and easily modify the notes on themes that I care most about. In the right sidebar (desktop users), you'll find a handful of such themes.


 
  
    
### Colophon 
This site was generated through [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and is hosted in Github. The prototyping and designing were done in my Campus B6 (6mm-ruled) notebook, Sarasa 0.5mm pens, and Figma.

I worked with HTML, CSS, and a smidge of Javascript for this static site, using VScode as a text editor. This is the first website I've made, so forgive the crudeness of the code.
