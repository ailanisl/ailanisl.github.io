This was a personal graphic design project to practice my photoshop skills. The idea was to put a modern twist on 70s bookcover design. 

<br>
<br>
## Poster of a Franz Kafka quote about rituals
Kafka is a guy who can write pithy but brain-turning one-liners. I encountered this quote in a [Jonathan Z. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Z._Smith) paper, and it has stuck with me since. 

<img src="/assets/img/ritual-poster.jpg" alt="Franz Kafka quote poster (Photoshop)">

## Poster of Borges' story "On the Exactitude of Science"
This is one of my favorite short stories, by one of my favorite authors. You can read more about Borges' work on this  <a href="/books/collected-fictions/">post</a>. As it is only a paragraph long story, there are no dedicated bookcover designs for it. So I decided to make my own:

# Notes 
## Typography 
The main typeface I used in this series is [LT Glockenspiel by LyonsType](https://www.dafont.com/lt-glockenspiel.font)
> "Glockenspiel" is a typeface that reflects on the art nouveau period in a unique magnitude, as well as the aesthetics of the 19th century. The typeface also takes cues from the works of Herman Ihlenburg, Schelter & Giesecke, etc. (from the author)
