__anodyne__: (adj.)
1. capable of soothing or eliminating pain

__compassion__: (noun)
1. Literally, 'suffering with'

__dovetail__: (verb)
1. To connect or combine precisely or harmoniously; into a unified whole

__florilegia__: (noun)
1. gathering flowers 
2. A collection of literature verses like a commonplace book. Private book of anecdote, poetry, daily thoughts, lists

__sublimate__: (intrans. verb) 
1. to be transformed directy from the solid to gaseous state or vice versa without liquid state (liminal state)
2. to divert the expression of (an instinctual desire or impulse) from its unacceptable form to one that is considered more socially or culturally acceptable 


__taxonomy__: (noun)
1. an ordered arrangement of groups or categories
