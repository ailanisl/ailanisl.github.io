## Chapters 1 - 2 
> _Enactive_ chosen to emphasize the growing conviction that cognition is not the representation of a pregiven world by a pregiven mind but is rather the enactment of a world and a mind on the basis of a history of the variety of actions that a being in the world performs. The enactive approach takes seriously, then, the philosophical critique of the idea that the mind is a mirror of nature but goes further by addressing this issue from within the heartland of science.

## 3. Symbols: The Cognitivist Hypothesies 


| Question posed | Cognitivist Answer |
| ---------------- | --------------- |
| What is cognition?|  Information processing as symbolic computation—rule-based manipulation of symbols. |
|How does it work? | Through any device that can support and manipulate discrete functional elements—the symbols. The system interacts only with the form of the symbols (their physical attributes), not their meaning. |
|  How do I know when a cognitive system is functioning adequately? | When the symbols appropriately represent some aspect of the real world, and the information processing leads to a successful solution of the problem given to the system. |


<br>
### Manifestations of Cognitivism
- [AI]({{ '/articles/artificial-intelligence/' | relative_url }}) - literal construal of the cognitivist hypothesis
- The study of natural, biologically implemented cognitive systems, especially humans 
    - Mental representations are taken to be occurrences of a formal system, and the mind’s activity is what gives these representations their attitudinal color— beliefs, desires, intentions, etc.
    - Here, therefore, unlike AI, we find an interest in what the natural cognitive systems are really like, and it is assumed that their cognitive representations are about something for the system

### Cognitivism and Neuroscience 
- Shaped current views of the brain 
    - Neurobiology mostly permeated with cognitivist, info-processing POV - unquestioningly 
- EX: Visual Cortex studies 
    -  Classify cortical neurons (cortical detectors) that respond to certain attributes of objects in the visual field (orientation, contrast, velocity, color, etc.) 
    -  Aligns with cognitivist hypothesis: 
        - Visual info → retina → feature-specific neurons in visual cortex → passed on to later stages in brain for further processing [Conceptual categorization, Memory associations, eventually Action]
