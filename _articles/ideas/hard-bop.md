I didn't like "jazz" until I heard hard-bop. The jazz I had associated with the word is cool jazz, which bored me half the time. Hard bop woke me up.

Reaction against the intellectualism in bebop. Incorporates RnB, gospel, and the blues. 

__Artists I like:__
- Horace Silver 
- Charles Mingus
- Art Blakely 
- Cannonball Adderley
- Hank Mobley 
- Thelonious Monk 
- Lee Morgan
