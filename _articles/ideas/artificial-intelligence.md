## __Generative vs Predictive AI__
__Generative AI__: AI that is used to _make_ things using existing data. 
- genuine technical advances so far

__Predictive AI__: AI that is used to _predict_ things using existing data.
- Less progress, different technology

> Predictive AI has riden the hype around Generative AI, unfairly. To make decisions based on AI predictions (in finance, education, justice system, hiring) is potentionally dangerous and will have societal ramifications. We cannot predict the future, outside some small domanins. (paraphrasing Sayash Kapoor on _Utopias_ podcast)

Data applied to people, individuals is impossible, because we do not know how to quantify human experience.
