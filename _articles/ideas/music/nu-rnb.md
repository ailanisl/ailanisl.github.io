Generally known plainly as "RnB" today. Grew out of New Jack Swing. 

Female dominance, but awesome male presence too (D'Angelo, Anthony Hamilton). Soul-infused ballads reigns, usually on topics of love, romantic wrongdoing, etc. 

Neo-soul: Soulquarians 

### My top artists:

- Erykah Badu 
- Lauryn Hill 
- SWV 
- Aaliyah 
- D'Angelo
- Anthony Hamilton
