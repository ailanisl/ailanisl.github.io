Perhaps my most listened to genre of music, and the music closest to my heart. The music my dad listened to.

### My top soul artists:
- Bill Withers 
- Curtis Mayfield
- The Neville Brothers
- Otis Redding 
- The O'Jays
- Gil Scott-Heron
- Ester Phillips
- Harold Melvin and the Blue Notes
- The Temptations
- Syl Johnson
