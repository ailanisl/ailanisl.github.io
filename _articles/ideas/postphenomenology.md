Technology as mediators of human experiences 

## Human-technology relations 
1. __Embodiment relations__: User's experience is reshaped by device, device taken into bodily awareness
2. __Hermeneutic Relations__: transform users' encounters with the world (e.g., wristwatch)
3. __Alterity Relations__: tech related to _as though_ it is human (AI, chatbox)
4. __Background Relations__: shares environmental context with humans
