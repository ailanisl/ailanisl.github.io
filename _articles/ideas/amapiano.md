Dance and music genre from South Africa. Originated in Joburg townships (Soweto, Pretoria)

At first, I didn't like it because it sounded too "house," but it grew on me. I think it was the contagious dances that go along with it - tshwala bam, mnike, umlado, hamba wena, all the bacardi. The dances are simple enough, but must be done with a sort of off-beat nonchalance that makes it look and feel cool. I only know the trendy songs, but apparently there's different sub-genres within amapiano (e.g. Private school piano, gqom 2.0, etc.). 

__Key characteristic:__ log drum (electronic simulation)

__Precursors:__ kwaito
