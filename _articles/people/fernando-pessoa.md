His technique: writing with dissociative personalities. His mind was catholic (lowercase) and, being full of everything, was fundamentally empty. His last name Pessoa (person) was an apt and generic placeholder. 

He was an accountant his whole life. Did his work, wrote on the side, barely published under his own name. 

Everything he wrote, those fragments, were found and published after his death. Yet another artist who became known only after dying (a trend among my favorite artists: see [Emily Dickinson]({{ '/articles/emily-dickinson/' | relative_url }}))

He's all over Lisbon, or so I hear.
