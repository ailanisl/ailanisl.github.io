_The following are my notes from a class on Chinese Buddhism (taught by Dr. Xiaoming Hou):_
## Etymology: 
_Vimala_ = absence of stains 
_Kirti_ = balance

## Portrayal of Vimalakirti: 
Merchant, eloquent, and wealthy. A father and a husband.
For the sake of maturing beings, he enters the most mundane places — gambling venues, pleasure houses, schools of heterodox teachers

Wore white clothes of layman, carried himself like consummate ascetic 

Left life of the householder, yet unaffected by any of this world’s three realms: the realm of desire (our realm), the realm of pure bodily form (subtle desires for attainment), and the incorporeal realm

Gave the appearance of having wives, children, and other women folk yet he remained celibate at all times 
- Doing whatever layman doing but not attached to desire

He appeared to subsist on food and drink yet at all time his nourishment came from meditative concentration 
- High spiritual attainment = no need for worldly subsistence 
- Normal food, drink considered worldly desires 
- Nourishment through touching 
    - Baby needs mothers touch 
- Pleasure nourished through dhyanic concentrations 

He showed up in all gaming houses, where he brought spiritual maturity, living beings addicted to the pleasures of gambling, yet he remained constantly vigilant

## Sutra Outline 
### Manifestation of Disease 
Due to his eloquence, no disciples of the Buddha, sravaka or bodhisattva, dare to pay him a visit...Finally, Bodhisattva Manjusri accepted the request (Considered to be the top bodhisattva in wisdom)

Manjusri asks why he’s ill…
- He is ill “because beings are ill due to ignorance and thirst for existence”
- Not really sick, but just Demonstrating the impermanence of the physical body
- Wants to create a chance for city folk to visit him and tell them about his teachings 
- Vimalakirti kept talking about how Sravakayana is not ultimate path 
Doctrine of praise and criticism 
- Insist on the superiority of the Mahayana and criticize the inferiority of Hinayana
- Not considered highest sutra in China

### Appearance of Nonduality
The goddess scatters flowers, which slide off the bodies of the bodhisattvas but stick to the bodies of the sravaka...because Sariputra still clings to discriminating perception (vikalpana) 
Her eloquence is due to her not having attained anything
__If you think that by practicing Buddhism, you are attaining anything (conceptual: there is an ‘i’ that is attaining a ‘thing’ = nirvana) then we are not practicing Mahayana__
Her eloquence results because she does not think this way 		
- Non-duality between a female and male body 


### The Gate of Non-duality 
Reason why we enter gate - dualistic appearance created by conceptual thought 
- This world by itself is as it is, when we start perceiving it, then we draw a line that does not exist (think there’s a subject that is separate from the object)
- Line created by discriminative thought 
- Drawing two pictures on different panels - create crease that separate the two pictures even though they depict one picture 
- Invisible line we use to divide our world that shapes basic perception, distortion
