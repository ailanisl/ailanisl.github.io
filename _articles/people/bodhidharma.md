Mythical founder of Chan lineage in China, came on request of 27th patriarch. Encounter between Bodhidharma and Emperor Wu of the Liang Dynasty

Bodhidharma crosses the river on a reed and sits in a cave for 9 years, facing the cave wall. A young man hears about him and goes to find him. He sits outside the cave for days. Finally, he cuts off his arm to present to Bodhidharma.
That got his attention, so the man asks him how to put his mind at rest. Bodhidharma responds, ‘Give me your mind.” The man understands at the moment (you cannot grasp the mind, so you cannot put it to rest) and he becomes the 2nd Chinese Patriarch.
