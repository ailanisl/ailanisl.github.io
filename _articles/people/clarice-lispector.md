Born Chaya Pinkhasivna Lispector, Clarice Lispector was a Ukrainian-born Brazilian writer of novels and stories that dwelled in the mystic mundanity of the upper-middle-class Brazilian woman. 

She was raised in Recife, in northeastern Brazil, and later lived in Rio de Janeiro. She attended law school and started publishing journalistic work. She was collecting her writings on miscellaneous papers, and, at the urging of a literary friend, published her first novel Near to the Wild Heart when she was 23 years old. She married a diplomat and left Brazil for life abroad. She had two sons. In addition to rearing her children, she devoted her time to writing more novels, an odd children’s story, and cronicas (Brazilian periodical columns). 

Clarice has her own form of grammar that denies the language of the rational mind. Her voice was the first that reverberated deep within the folds of my being.
