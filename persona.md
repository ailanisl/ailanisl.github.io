Human who is (and will probably always be) trying-to-figure-it-out. For now: I’m studying design with an interest in the poetic and ambient web. 

My practice is, in a word, thoughtful. I’m a slow thinker, but I’m deliberate with my intentions and research.

My explorations in Buddhist philosophies, modernist literature and poetry, and 4E cognition inform my practices and ideals in design.

Studied embodied cognition and HCI at UC Berkeley (2025) with a minor in Buddhist Studies. 

My strengths: 
- **Eye for traditional design aesthetics**: from ancient Japanese aesthetics and Art Nouveau, to abstract and surrealist artforms, I believe novelization depends on the syncreticization of history 

- **Research**: I delve deep into topics to create authentic experiences. I’ve received academic awards and fellowships for humanities and cognitive linguistics research. 

- **Storytelling**: Since middle school, I’ve always found joy in presenting information through slideshows that employ creative visual metaphors. Perhaps my best skill is writing and playing with language in unconventional ways.
