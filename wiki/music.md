I listen to music for...
>  1. Dance (in which case I _need_ polyrhythms and offbeat)
>  2. Lyrics and songwriting 
>  3. To match my melancholy (I listen to sad music when I am most content)

For me, __genre__ is important.

My playlist curation is based on genre, or specific genre-fusions. 

<i> My mood dictates the genre, the genre dictates the playlist. </i>
