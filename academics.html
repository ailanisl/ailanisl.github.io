BIO

  1. I graduated from UC Berkeley with a bachelor's in cognitive science and a minor in Buddhist studies. There, I did some independent (idiosyncratic) research on how conceptual metaphor may have accounted for the distinctly modern and individualistic model of mind in American Buddhism, which pervades current mindfulness movements and neuroscience research of meditation.

    I also did some lab and research apprenticeships during my time at Berkeley. I studied how far-right groups reasoned about climate change through religious metaphor (e.g., altar of climate, St. Greta) with Eve Sweetser and Kim Grogan (UBC). I also helped out at Berkeley's Language and Cognitive Development Lab, where we looked at how children determine group belonging based on language cues and how Hindu and Muslim children in Gujarat, India thought about norms, violations, and loopholes of religious groups (their own and others).
