BIO

I'm a PhD student in Cognitive Psychology and Language Science, working with Dr. Emiko Muraki at Penn State University. I'm interested in cognitive semantics (the cognitive science of meaning), and how cognition and language are like symbiotic parasites on our bodily, emotional, cultural, and environmental systems.

Currently, I'm interested in looking at the interaction of vocal iconicity (the resemblance between sound and meaning), crossmodal imagery, and multisensory integration.

I was born and raised in Los Angeles, California, specifically the San Gabriel Valley. I love the culture in SoCal, from the bandas that play late into the night, the unassuming strip malls with the best fusion cuisines, to the bright green parrots squawking on the powerlines.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

I'm excited by some big questions that straddle the disciplines of cognitive science:

  1. What aspects and combinations of sensory & bodily experiences become meaningful to humans?
  2. Why do certain sounds, gestures, and movements become signals, signs, and words (and eventually abstract concepts) in the cognitive system?
  3. How does this interplay with our embeddedness in a social, cultural, and ecological world?
  4. How do differences in subjective and linguistic experiences change our cognition?
  5. What impact does play and ludic activities have in language use, development, and/or evolution?
