title: Listening Is not Reading

The other day, I heard a story on NPR asking if listening to audiobooks counts as reading. My take is that they do not. But my take is idiosyncratic, in that I only know that it applies to me.  

This, of course, gets into the conversation of the diversity of learning styles – whether one learns better visually, kinesthetically, aurally, or other. But, then again, reading, to me, is not necessarily associated with _learning_ per se. So, first I must break down why I read.

__Reasons I read__ (listed not in importance but in the order they come to mind): 
- Escape and enjoyment, like watching Netflix 
- Learning, through nonfiction books 
- The aesthetic experience
